Numbers worth having ready

  • One service, one hop from the shop. scout-service listens on :3300 on the internal Docker network with traefik.enable=false. The only way in is the shop's /api/scout route, which sets userId from the better-auth cookie.
  • 19 tools, at most 5 rounds. Round zero forces a tool choice, with respond_to_user as the "no tool" sentinel. MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 5, and a sixth call is an error, not a retry.
  • Spend is capped before the model is called. $1 per conversation and $50 per 24 hours for the whole store, priced per model id in scout_llm_call.
  • Memory recall is best-effort. It returns the top 5 by pgvector distance over 1536-dimension text-embedding-3-small vectors, with a 2 s budget. A failure yields no memories, not a failed turn.
  • The turn outlives the socket. SSE heartbeats every 10 s, a card waits up to 5 minutes, and a finished channel is collected about 60 s after its last subscriber. The ring buffer is process memory, so it survives a dropped connection but not a restart.
  • The model can be swapped per request. The default today is gemini-2.5-flash, a July stopgap while the OpenAI account was out of credits (WI-5324). gpt-4o-mini is the intended default. Groq and Ollama sit behind the same adapter, with 3 tries on 429/5xx.

01 · What Scowtt does

First-party CRM data turned into better ad signals

Scowtt predicts which leads will convert and how valuable they will be, from a customer's own CRM data. It sends those predictions back to Google, Meta and other ad platforms as conversion signals, so campaigns optimize toward revenue instead of form fills. Marketers keep their existing tools.

Three products

Predictive modeling: scores leads by likelihood and value of conversion. AI marketing: pushes those scores into ad platforms automatically. AI sales agent: talks to high-value leads to book calls or appointments.

Stage and people

Founded 2024; Seattle (Crunchbase lists Kirkland, WA). $12M Series A in Dec 2025, led by Inspired Capital with LiveRamp Ventures. At the time: about 10 people and $3.2M ARR. CEO Eduardo Indacochea spent 13+ years at Microsoft, then held leadership roles at Google and Meta, where he was a VP in advertising.

Results they publish

LaserAway +59% purchase ROAS; Advanced Hair Restoration +64%; a large education advertiser +38% ROAS by modeling likelihood to enroll. Customers are lead-gen, performance-driven advertisers on search and social.

Where they are going

An AI "operating system for growth" that connects marketing and sales through the data already in the CRM. The Data Platform is what makes that possible, so this hire is foundational.

02 · Read between the lines

The job is making customer onboarding fast and pipelines trustworthy

Every new customer arrives with different CRM objects, custom fields, warehouses and identifiers. Their "what success looks like" list starts with customer data onboarding becomes much faster and needs less manual work. That is the problem to talk about.

Sources

Stack

05 · The likely whiteboard

“Onboard a new customer's CRM data and send value signals to Google and Meta”

Walk through the design in this order. Name the trade-offs as you go; that is what a Head of Engineering is listening for.

Clarify

Which CRM and objects (leads, opportunities, custom fields)? Volume? How fresh must signals be? Does the customer give API access or a warehouse share?

Ingest

Connectors pull incrementally with a watermark (e.g. Salesforce SystemModstamp / updated_at) and a separate backfill path. Land raw data append-only, per tenant, in GCS/BigQuery so you can replay it.

Normalize

Map each customer's schema to a canonical lead → opportunity → outcome model using config, not code. That mapping layer is the onboarding-speed lever. Detect schema drift and alert instead of failing silently.

Identity and quality

Join on click IDs (gclid, fbclid) and normalized, hashed email/phone. Check row counts, null rates, duplicates and freshness per tenant before scoring.

Score

Build curated feature tables shared by training and inference so they can't drift apart; the model outputs P(convert) and expected value.

Deliver

Upload conversions/values via Google Ads offline or enhanced conversions and the Meta Conversions API. Use idempotency keys (lead ID + event + time) so retries never double-count. Retry with backoff and send failures to a dead-letter queue you can replay.

Observe

Per-tenant dashboards and alerts on freshness, match rate, upload acceptance rate and score distribution shift. An onboarding validation report the customer-success team can read.

Security

Strict tenant isolation, least-privilege credentials per customer, hash PII before it leaves, and audit who accessed what.

Say one thing you'd not build yet (e.g. real-time streaming everywhere) and why. Staff engineers are judged on what they choose to leave out.

06 · Short answers to rehearse

Likely questions

A system you owned end to end

Papercusp coordination: the problem, what you built, one hard failure, how you found the root cause, and what guard stops it recurring.

A production incident

Pick one where the first explanation was wrong. Show how you measured instead of guessing, then fixed the class of bug, not just the one instance.

Ambiguous problem, no spec

How you scoped it, what you shipped first, how you knew it was working, and what you deliberately deferred.

Backfill vs incremental

Same transformation code for both; backfills are chunked, resumable and throttled; incremental runs use watermarks with a lookback window for late updates; outputs are idempotent upserts.

Schema evolution

Keep the raw data; version the mapping; additive changes flow through, while breaking changes alert and quarantine instead of corrupting downstream tables.

Why a 10–20 person startup

You want ownership and to see results directly. You've operated a business, so revenue-linked engineering appeals to you.

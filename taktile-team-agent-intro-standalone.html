01 · Before you dial

Settle these before the call

A recruiter screen is mostly a logistics check. If you hedge on location or pay here, the process can stall without anyone telling you why.

Location · the big one

Team Agent is listed in Europe only

The posting's locations are Berlin (HQ), London, Iași and Dublin, 3 days a week in the office. Taktile's only US hub is New York, and the Agents team isn't listed there. You're a US citizen moving to San Francisco. Pick one of these before 12:00:

  • A · Relocating to Europe. Only say this if it's true. Then ask about relocation and work-permit support, since Taktile says sponsorship depends on role and location.
  • B · US only. Ask straight out whether the Agents team hires in New York or remotely in the US. If it doesn't, ask to be considered for the NYC roles below.
If B · the US roles

What's open in New York right now

Sr. Applied AI Engineer: builds reusable GenAI agents for credit and financial crime, acting as the product owner for each agent. That's the closest match to Papercusp.

Senior Forward Deployed Engineer: leads customer deployments of production AI agents in Python and SQL.

Taktile asks candidates to name a top priority if they're in more than one process (maximum of 3). NYC is still hybrid, 3 days a week, and you'll be in SF, so be upfront about that too.

Pay

Keep it consistent with Acadian

Yesterday you told Acadian's recruiter you're targeting $300K and would go as low as $200K for the right fit. If Eva asks, give a US-based range that matches that, and ask what the band is for the entity you'd be hired through (Taktile LLC is the US entity). Don't anchor on a Berlin number.

Taktile in a minute

Customers combine AI agents, rules, context and human oversight to automate decisions: approving loans, paying claims, stopping fraud, AML reviews. Customers include Mercury, Monzo, Faire and Pleo. Reported results: 95% automation in B2B underwriting and 75% fewer AML false positives.

The funds go to AI products for complex banking and insurance cases and to US, EMEA and LATAM growth. The CEO told Fortune “2026 is the year where AI comes to financial services.”

Founders

Two Harvard PhDs from QuantCo

Maik Taro Wehmeyer (CEO) and Maximilian Eber met at Harvard and worked together at QuantCo. There they kept rebuilding the same decision logic for every client, so they founded Taktile in 2020. 05 · Conversation hooks

Their engineering blog is about the problems you've been solving

31 Jul 2026

“How we taught our AI coworker to take no for an answer”

“Claudius” is an internal agent: “about 50 GitHub Actions workflows in a trenchcoat.” It runs on Opus 5, uses about 30B input tokens a month (about $30K), and does 2,000–3,600 runs a week. It wrote roughly 63% of the commits in its own repo. The lesson: a PR that's closed without merging is the most valuable feedback, so its rule is now “Closed = declined, don't resubmit.”

Your parallel: in Papercusp a declined owner instruction has to carry a reason and is never silently retried. Agents are also stopped from bothering the human with questions they could answer themselves.

13 Jul 2026

“We gave our agent a memory”

They swapped RAG for an LLM-maintained wiki and compared grep, BM25, vector search and LLM judgment. BM25 was the “pragmatic middle”; an RRF hybrid reached 0.90 recall. Answering from the wiki was 86% faster and 61% cheaper. The open problem is staleness, and their planned fix is a per-source fingerprint.

Your parallel: Papercusp's memory and recipe search is a BM25-plus-embedding hybrid fused with RRF. Work-item checkpoints store a subject fingerprint, so a stale note is flagged instead of trusted. That is their planned fix, already running.

How to use these

One mention, as a question

Eva is a recruiter, so don't give a technical lecture. Try: “I read the Claudius post. I've run into the same ‘closed means declined’ problem with my own agents. Is that the Agents team's work, or a separate internal tool?” That shows you did the reading, and her answer tells you whether this team builds agents for customers or for internal use.

09 · How they hire

Four stages and six values

  1. Introductory call (today): background, goals, logistics
  2. Hiring manager: experience, problem-solving, teamwork
  3. Skills assessment: technical interview, case study or challenge. “The goal is not to develop a perfect solution.” May include people from other teams.
  4. Values & motivation with a founder or senior leader, then references

  • Desire for impact
